NON-EXEC

DARREN MAGEE

SCIENTIFIC OFFICER

Non-Executive Director / Shareholder · Scientific Officer & Decontamination Lead

A dedicated family man, former Royal Air Force Biomedical Engineer and award-winning inventor. Recipient of the UK Government Department of Trade and Industry SMART Award for innovation in medical devices and industrial injury prevention.

Associate Director of Medical Physics and Clinical Engineering at GESH (St George's, Epsom & St Helier University Hospitals NHS Trust). In 2022, his company was a finalist at the World Sustainability Awards for air pollution control work.

Active STEM Ambassador and Engineering Council Neighbourhood Engineer since 2006. Chair and Founder of ESTA (The Earth Science and Technology Association). Medico-Technical Advisor to Killing Cancer charity and PDT work.

His latest invention represents a potential step-change in in vivo technology — a safe, painless alternative to surgery, radiotherapy and chemotherapy with potential for inactivating blood-borne microorganisms including HIV, hepatitis and COVID-19.
