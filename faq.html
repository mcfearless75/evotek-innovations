SAFETY & ENVIRONMENT
The process is food-safe and water-based. It uses a natural acid formulation with no harmful residues. It has been independently tested and approved for use in sensitive environments including food preparation areas and healthcare settings.
Our process is water-based and made with a natural acid. The surfactants contained comply with the biodegradability criteria laid down in Regulation EC 648/2004. This ensures minimal environmental impact and aligns with NHS Green Plan sustainability priorities.
The applicator is specially designed to the exact micron, ensuring treatment particles don't linger in the air. This ensures respiratory safety and a fast 5–8 minute drying time. Standard fogging leaves airborne particles that pose respiratory risk; our SST process bonds directly to surfaces.
PERFORMANCE & DURATION
SST bonds to any substrate and reduces contamination adhesion across fabrics, metals, plastics, wood, glass, and porous surfaces. It is suited to any sector where surface hygiene, material durability, and reduced contamination build-up are priorities — from healthcare and hospitality to food service and public transport.
COMPLIANCE & STANDARDS
The surfactants contained comply with the biodegradability criteria laid down in Regulation EC 648/2004. This is the European standard for surfactant biodegradability in detergents and cleaning products.
Complies with Modified DIN EN 1040, Modified DIN EN 1276, and EN 14476. Also recognised with international standards ISO 9001 (Quality Management) and ISO 14001 (Environmental Management). EvoTek holds NHS supply partner status.
PRACTICAL APPLICATION
