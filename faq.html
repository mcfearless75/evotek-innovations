SECTORS & APPLICATIONS
All public sectors including Healthcare, Workplace, Hotels, Leisure, Entertainment, Catering and Public Transport with high traffic of public. The system prevents cross-contamination risk from surfaces, making it ideal anywhere people gather.

SAFETY & ENVIRONMENT
The process is food-safe and water-based. It uses a natural acid formulation with no harmful residues. It has been independently tested and approved for use in sensitive environments including food preparation areas and healthcare settings.
Our process is water-based and made with a natural acid. The surfactants contained comply with the biodegradability criteria laid down in Regulation EC 648/2004. This ensures minimal environmental impact and aligns with NHS Green Plan sustainability priorities.
The applicator is specially designed to the exact micron, ensuring disinfectant particles don't linger in the air. This ensures respiratory safety and a fast 5–10 minute drying time. Standard fogging leaves airborne particles that pose respiratory risk; our SST process bonds directly to surfaces.
PATHOGEN COVERAGE
Bacteria, Fungi and Viruses. The SST coating provides broad-spectrum efficacy across diverse microbial classes including hospital-acquired infection pathogens, antibiotic-resistant bacteria, all enveloped viruses as defined in EN 14476, and fungal species prevalent in healthcare environments.
UK-based BluTest Laboratories completed the latest Gold Standard COVID-19 Vaccinia testing in April 2020. The product achieved Log4, resulting in a 99.99% viricidal kill score. It is effective against all enveloped viruses as defined in EN 14476:2013 + A2:2019 Annex A, including all Coronaviruses and SARS-CoV-2.
COMPLIANCE & STANDARDS
The surfactants contained comply with the biodegradability criteria laid down in Regulation EC 648/2004. This is the European standard for surfactant biodegradability in detergents and cleaning products.
Complies with Modified DIN EN 1040, Modified DIN EN 1276, and EN 14476. Also recognised with international standards ISO 9001 (Quality Management) and ISO 14001 (Environmental Management). EvoTek holds NHS supply partner status.
