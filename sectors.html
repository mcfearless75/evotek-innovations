EvoTek's Smart Surface Technology (SST) is a universal platform — the same physics-driven Engineered Surface Physics (ESP) that protects an NHS ward also protects a family home, a hotel lobby, a gym changing room, or a fleet of buses. Wherever surfaces exist, SST delivers continuous, non-toxic, sustainable protection.

HEALTH & CARE
EvoTek's Smart Surface Technology (SST) delivers continuous protection for up to 336 hours between applications, dramatically reducing the cleaning hours, labour pressure, and chemical consumption normally required to maintain safe care environments. Because SST works through Engineered Surface Physics (ESP) rather than harsh chemistry, each application delivers up to 30× more value than traditional chemical treatments, which are toxic, short-lived, and lose efficacy within minutes of drying. Our physics-enhanced surface characteristics create environments that actively resist staining, repel liquids, and minimise the adhesion of residues and biofilm-forming deposits. By keeping surfaces cleaner, drier, and less favourable to microbial settlement, SST reduces odours linked to organic build-up, incontinence, footwear, and high-traffic clinical areas — supporting safer, fresher, and more dignified care settings without chemical release or corrosive agents. This is the future of hygiene: continuous, non-toxic, sustainable protection that strengthens best-practice cleaning and reduces the environmental conditions associated with disease transmission and AMR.
In care environments, EvoTek SST delivers 336 hours of continuous protection, reducing cleaning man-hours, labour strain, and chemical consumption by up to 30×. Applied via fine-mist atomisation, SST covers large areas quickly and economically, creating surfaces that repel spills, resist staining, and prevent the build-up of odours linked to incontinence, bedding, footwear, and shared spaces. The self-cleaning behaviour supports consistent hygiene between scheduled cleans while extending the lifespan of furnishings and equipment. This is a non-biocidal, resident-safe, dignity-enhancing approach to care-home hygiene.
HOME & LIVING
For families, SST brings hospital-grade surface protection into everyday life — but without chemicals, fumes, or risks. By modifying surface behaviour at the molecular level, our ESP-based coatings keep household surfaces cleaner for longer, reduce odours, prevent staining, and make kitchens, bathrooms, bedrooms, and children's spaces easier to maintain. This is proactive home hygiene: safer for children, pets, and allergy-sensitive individuals, while reducing reliance on harsh cleaning products. SST creates a healthier living environment by lowering the surface conditions that support microbial spread, giving families a cleaner, fresher, and more protected home — continuously.
EvoTek SST transforms everyday living spaces into cleaner, safer, fresher environments without chemical release, fragrances, or toxic agents. Our ESP-engineered coatings deliver stain-proofing, water-repellency, and self-cleaning behaviour, preventing the build-up of residues that cause odours from pets, cooking, smoke, footwear, and soft furnishings. By keeping surfaces drier and less adhesive, SST reduces the conditions that support microbial spread while extending the life of household materials. This is continuous, preventative home hygiene that protects families, saves money, and keeps living spaces healthier — all through pure physics.
